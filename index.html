What is Budgity?

Budgity is a financial planning app designed to be an easy to use, yet powerful tool for budgeting. Using a node based system, the app displays simple and comprehensive metrics that can help explain how and where to save money, makng budgeting simpiler. Our Budgity team wishes you happy budgeting!

Does it cost anything?

Budgity and all its software is free to use! We don’t charge our users anything. No future charges or hidden fees, we promise. Budgity is also ad-free. We prioritize your user experience above all else.

Why should you join?

Budgity simplifies budgeting to make it as stress-free as possible. With a simple and clutter free interface, a safe and secure system, and a no-cost sign-up, Budgity will be the only budgeting software you will ever need.
